I work at the intersection of software and practical AI, with a focus on real problems, clear thinking, and useful outcomes. This is a space to build, explore, and share what I learn along the way.

About this build

This website is the first VomerLabs project: a place to show useful work, explain how it is made, and share what I learn. It is being built in the open, starting with the foundation and homepage.

The approach

Astro generates the pages as static HTML. Projects and lab notes live in Markdown or MDX files, with schemas checking their metadata. Plain CSS stays scoped to the component that owns it, with a small shared set of design tokens.

What works today

The content collections, validation, shared layout, and homepage are in place. The homepage introduces the studio and provides a direct way to get in touch. No CMS, database, or client framework is needed.

What’s next

Dedicated project pages, a Lab Notes index, and the remaining site pages will follow. More work and writing will be added when they are ready to share.
