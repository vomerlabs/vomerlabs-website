## What it is

The VomerLabs website is the lab’s first project, it is a place to show useful projects, explain how they are made, and share what I learn.

## What’s inside

- **Home** Introduction of the the lab, project showcase, and lab notes.
- **Projects.** The work the lab has built, with its current status and a write-up.
- **Lab Notes.** Build notes and decisions, experiments, tools, problems and ideas, and discoveries.
- **About.** The lab’s approach and the principles behind it.
- **Contact.** A direct email link, with a few prompts to help you start.

## What works today

The main pages are built. Everything is working, the site is responsive and works on all browsers and devices.

## Where to find it

The site is live at [vomerlabs.com](https://vomerlabs.com/). The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/vomerlabs/vomerlabs-website).

## What’s next

More projects and notes will be added as they’re ready.
